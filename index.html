From Infinite Dimensions to Black Hole Universes: Refuting the Big Bang Theory

Published in The Astrophysical Journal. Author: Debjit Adhikari.

Abstract: General Relativity intrinsically predicts its own breakdown at the Big Bang singularity. The Borde-Guth-Vilenkin theorem rigorously demonstrates that any spacetime with an average expansion rate greater than zero must be geodesically incomplete in past null and timelike directions.
